Player Tool Guide / Start here

The dashboard introduces your tools. Open a tool below, enter the appropriate information, and save when your account and membership are active.

  1. Player Next Move: guided, rules-based suggestions for a basketball question; it is not a live coach or medical advice.
  2. Goals + Grind, Workout Tracker, My Numbers: plan, record work, and review progress. Official team stats remain coach-controlled.
  3. Highlights + Film: save your video links and review notes. This tool does not upload or host video files.
  4. Basketball Passport: your season-by-season team, coach, and milestone timeline.
  5. Opportunities and College Pathway: organize research and next steps with an adult.
  6. Bounce Back and Downloads: reflect on setbacks and take your records with you.
  7. My Saved Articles: open a saved guide to view or update your private highlights and journal note.

The free Training Lab, Basketball Library, and article reading are available without membership. Under-18 enrollment remains unavailable until verified guardian approval is ready.
